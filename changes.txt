= Health bar now counts Absorption. While any is left, the whole text (percentage, health/max, armor) turns yellow and absorption is added to both sides: what's left goes on top of health, the full pool for the effect's level goes on top of max health (20 HP + Absorption I = 20+8 / 28), so the bar stays at or under 100% instead of looking like it broke the limit. The Script API can't read absorption, so systems/movement.ts tracks it: the effect sets the pool to 8 HP at level I plus 2 HP per extra level, each hit drains the pool first, and a 6-tick check clears it when the effect ends or restores it after a rejoin.
